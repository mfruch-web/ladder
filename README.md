# Ladder

A daily word ladder. Change one letter at a time to get from the start word to the target word, in as few steps as you can. Three puzzles a day: Easy, Medium and Hard.

It's a static site with no build step and no server: every player's browser generates the same puzzles from the date. Sister game to [Pipeflow](https://playpipeflow.com), built on the same patterns.

## Run it locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```

### Test on your iPhone
1. Make sure your Mac and iPhone are on the same Wi-Fi.
2. Run the server above, then find your Mac's IP: `ipconfig getifaddr en0`
3. On the iPhone, open Safari and go to `http://<that-ip>:8000`

Handy URL flags (for testing only):
- `?date=2026-11-28` plays another day's puzzles (stats aren't recorded)
- `?play=easy` / `?play=medium` / `?play=hard` skips the splash screen

To reset your progress: Safari → Settings → Advanced → Website Data, then delete the site.

## Tests

```sh
node test/puzzles.test.js   # a full year of puzzles at every level (about 10 seconds)
node test/ui.mjs            # plays the game in headless Chrome on iPhone SE → Pro Max sizes
```

The puzzle test checks that generation is fast and deterministic, par is in range and is the true shortest path, every word on the par path is a common word, no offensive word appears, blocked words aren't accepted, start and target aren't trivially similar, Easy always has several routes, and puzzles don't repeat. The UI test checks layout (no sideways scroll, keyboard and target on screen, touch-target sizes) in light and dark mode, and plays the tutorial, a solve with a mistake, undo and hint, a give-up, a long Hard ladder, the share text, reload persistence and the streak. Screenshots go to `test/screenshots/`.

## How it works

- `js/words.js`: the word lists (generated, see below).
- `js/puzzle.js`: the engine. Builds the word graph (words one letter apart are linked), finds shortest paths with breadth-first search, and picks each day's puzzles with a seeded RNG.
- `js/game.js`: the UI, custom keyboard, hints, scoring, stats and sharing.

### Levels
| Level | Letters | Par | Extra rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 4 | 3–4 | Every step fixes one more letter, and there are at least 3 different shortest routes |
| Medium | 4 (5 on about a third of days) | 5 (4–5 for five-letter words) | |
| Hard | 5 | 6–7 | |

On a rare day when nothing fits, Medium and Hard may go one past their par range. Change levels in `LEVELS` in `puzzle.js`.

### Par and common words
There are two word graphs over the same words. **Par** is the shortest path through *every* accepted word, so nobody can beat par with an obscure shortcut. A puzzle is only used if a route that short exists using **common words only**, so the intended solution never needs an obscure word. Hints and Give up follow that common route.

### No repeats
No server and no history, so variety comes from the date. Start words and target words come from separate pools, each word has a slot in a repeating cycle (53 days for starts, 7 for targets), and the two cycles only line up once every 371 days. So:
- a start word comes back at most every 53 days, and no start or target word comes back within 7 days;
- the same start-and-target pair can't come back within a year on a normal day. A few days per level per year need a last-resort fallback, which leaves a tiny chance of a repeat (about one in four years in testing); the test allows at most one a year.
- In later years each day also avoids the pairs its matching days in earlier years used, where it has another good option.

Details are in the comments in `puzzle.js`.

**Changing the word lists or the generator changes every puzzle**, past and future. After launch, bump `VERSION` in `puzzle.js` and only make changes you're happy to see take effect from the next day.

### Scoring
Your score is the number of steps in your finished ladder plus 1 per hint, compared with par. Taking back a step is free. Going back to a word you already used cuts out the loop. Share squares are graded by percent over par (0 / 20 / 40 / 70 / 100%), since pars are small. Give up shows a shortest route; it counts as played but not solved. Your streak continues on any day you solve at least one puzzle.

Hints ask for a second tap so a stray tap next to M doesn't cost a step. `useHint` in `js/game.js` marks where a "watch an ad for a hint" option would go.

## Word lists

`js/words.js` is generated by `tools/build-words.js`:

```sh
sh tools/fetch-sources.sh   # downloads the sources into tools/sources/ (not committed)
node tools/build-words.js   # writes js/words.js and review lists in tools/review/
```

Three tiers per word length:
- **accepted** (about 3,900 four-letter and 8,600 five-letter words): any valid guess. ENABLE plus SCOWL up to size 60, American spelling.
- **common** (about 1,540 / 2,500): everyday words. In ENABLE, SCOWL size 35 or smaller, and frequent in everyday speech (subtitle word counts). Every word on a par route comes from here.
- **core** (about 940 / 1,100): the most common base words (no plurals or past tenses), the only words used as a start or target.

Hand-made lists in `tools/lists/`:
- `block-all.txt`: slurs, sexual and crude words. Never accepted, not even as a guess. The LDNOOBW list is applied on top, minus the everyday words in `allow-guess.txt`. Anything containing a few roots (e.g. the f-word) is blocked outright.
- `block-puzzle.txt`: fine as a guess but never in a puzzle: crude second meanings, mild profanity, insults, drugs, grim topics.
- `common-remove.txt`: removed from the common list after a full manual read-through (archaic, obscure, British-only, contractions like CANT/WONT, proper nouns).
- `core-remove.txt`: fine on a route but too dull or odd as a start or target (function words, Greek letters, and so on).
- `common-add.txt`: everyday words the frequency data missed.

## iPhone details

- Custom on-screen keyboard, so the iOS keyboard never pops up, covers the screen or autocorrects.
- Fits every iPhone from the SE (375×667) to the Pro Max, and stays clear of the notch, Dynamic Island and home indicator. A long ladder scrolls while the target and keyboard stay put.
- No double-tap zoom, pinch zoom, rubber-band bounce, long-press callout or text-selection loupe.
- Haptic feedback (iOS 18+), the timer pauses when the app goes to the background, and an old tab reopened the next day loads the new puzzles. Each day's puzzles are cached so they load instantly.
- Works as a home-screen app with its own icon. After a solve, Safari users get a one-time tip about adding it.
- On a computer you can type: press 1–5 or click a letter to pick a spot, or type a whole word and press Enter. Backspace takes back a step.

## Deploy

**Live at https://playwordladders.com**, hosted on Cloudflare (Workers static assets, domain registered with Cloudflare) and connected to this repo, so every push to `main` deploys automatically. The old `ladder.mfrucht33.workers.dev` address, `www`, and plain `http` all forward to the domain (the script at the top of `index.html` and `privacy.html`), so saved stats and streaks live at one address.

1. If the domain ever changes, update it in that redirect script, the canonical and link-preview tags in `index.html` (they need full URLs or iMessage won't show the image), and `siteUrl` in `js/config.js` (share text).
2. Set `launchDate` in `js/config.js` to the day puzzle #1 goes live.
3. Fill in the placeholders in `privacy.html`.

## Ads (Google AdSense)

1. Apply at adsense.google.com once the site is live. Approval goes better with real traffic, a privacy policy (included) and an About/contact page.
2. Once approved, put your `ca-pub-…` client ID and slot IDs in `js/config.js`. The two ad slots, one in the results card and one below a finished puzzle, appear automatically.
3. Add an `ads.txt` file at your site root with the line AdSense gives you.
4. For visitors in the EU and UK you'll need a consent banner. AdSense's built-in "Privacy & messaging" tool handles this.

Ads never appear next to the keyboard or the ladder, which keeps misclicks (and AdSense policy problems) away.
