How to play

Get from the start word to the target word by changing one letter at a time.

COLDCORDWORDWARDWARM
  • Tap a letter in your word, then tap a new letter on the keyboard.
  • Every step has to be a real word. You can't add, remove or move letters.
  • Letters that already match the target turn green.
  • ↶ takes back your last step. Going back to a word you've already used cuts out the loop.
  • Stuck? Hint fills in the next word (+1 step), or Give up shows a shortest route.

Scoring

Each puzzle has a par: the fewest steps it can be done in. You're scored on how many steps your finished ladder takes, like golf. Taking back a step is free; each hint adds 1.

Your share squares show how close you came: 🟩🟩🟩🟩 is par, and each 🟨 means more steps over par (up to 20%, 40%, 70%, then 100% over).

Every day

Everyone gets the same three ladders each day: Easy (4 letters), Medium and Hard (5 letters). New ones arrive at midnight.

On a computer you can also type: click a letter or press 1–5 to pick a spot, or type a whole word and press Enter. Backspace takes back a step.
